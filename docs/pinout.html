Smart Display · wiring

ST7789 display and touch button on an ESP32-S3 Super Mini

All eight display wires come from the right-hand pad row of the Super Mini: power on top, then GPIO 13 down to GPIO 8. Every wire has its own colour in the diagram and the table.

ST7789 · 240×280 · SPI SPI 40 MHz · HSPI 3.3 V supply 8 wires · one pad row

Touch button

ModuleSuper MiniFirmwareNote
TTP223 · OUTGPIO 6TOUCH_PIN=6Digital input: HIGH = touch. The ESP32 capacitive sensor is not enabled on this pin.
TTP223 · VCC3V3—Module supply
TTP223 · GNDGND—Common ground

Short tap — next mode. Hold 2 s — menu; in the menu a short tap moves to the next item and a 1 s hold selects it (the bar at the bottom turns white; no need to let go). No TTP223? Set TOUCH_IS_TTP223 = false in Config.h and solder a bare wire or copper pad to a capacitive-touch GPIO.

Display settings in firmware

  • ST7789 driver, 240×280, portrait (setRotation(0)).
  • Colour inversion on, BGR order.
  • The controller has 240×320 RAM; the library applies the 20-row offset itself when the height is 280.
  • The 240×280 frame and a copy of the background live in PSRAM, 134 KB each.

Avoid

  • Powering VCC from 5V.
  • GPIO 0, 45 and 46 for the display: they are strapping pins and can stop the board from booting.
  • GPIO 48 — it drives the on-board WS2812 LED.
  • Selecting 16 MB flash: this board has 4 MB, and a wrong size bootloops before Serial starts.

platformio.ini excerpt

-DST7789_DRIVER=1
-DTFT_WIDTH=240
-DTFT_HEIGHT=280
-DTFT_RGB_ORDER=TFT_BGR
-DTFT_INVERSION_ON=1
-DSPI_FREQUENCY=40000000
-DTFT_CS=13   -DTFT_DC=12   -DTFT_MOSI=11
-DTFT_SCLK=10 -DTFT_BL=9    -DTFT_BACKLIGHT_ON=HIGH
-DTFT_RST=8   -DTFT_MISO=-1
Flash with pio run -t upload. On first boot the board starts the SmartDisplay access point; the setup page is at http://192.168.4.1.
